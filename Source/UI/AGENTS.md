# Source/UI/

UI components for displaying certainty and ideo opinion data.

- `CertaintyBar.cs` — reusable certainty bar widget; `DrawThreshold` draws the crisis threshold line, `DrawTargetMarker` draws the setpoint marker, `Tooltip` builds the setpoint/band tooltip; used by both the `SocialCardUtility_DrawCertainty` patch and `OpinionCard`, so the two cannot drift apart
- `OpinionCard.cs` — one pawn's stance list and opinion of each known ideo; the constructor (run every frame) refreshes the shown pawn through `RecacheBaseOpinionsIfStale`/`RecacheSetpointIfStale`, then does layout (`Size`); `Draw()` paints at the group origin; `CanShow` gates both tabs; stance rows cover both faiths' issues plus the pawn's own held stances above strength 1; text colour grades each stance against the selected faith (green/red), a left gutter stripe grades it against the pawn's trait target (green aligned, red conflict; `IssueStanceTracker.TraitTargets`, one call per frame), ◀/▶ before the rung marks a rung change over the past day (toward the permissive/forbidding end), and ▲/▼ before the strength marks a strength change (`IssueStanceTracker.StanceBefore`); a large swing (≥2 rungs, ≥3 strength) draws two overlapping glyphs (`DrawArrow`: side by side for rungs, stacked for strength), and the arrow slots are sized from the measured glyph width; the row tooltip adds a line for each shown arrow (old → new rung or strength) and what the pawn's traits oppose and the stance they drift toward; per-issue breakdown visible in dev mode
- `ITab_Opinion.cs` — pawn inspect tab; thin host for `OpinionCard`
- `WITab_Caravan_Opinion.cs` — caravan world tab (patched in after Social); lists pawns with an ideo, and docks the selected pawn's `OpinionCard` beside the tab like `WITab_Caravan_Social`
